About the app

A toolkit that keeps the controls visible.

Masterkit Injector helps organize locally stored visual assets such as skins, recalls, emotes, maps, and trails. The experience focuses on clean navigation, clear permissions, and accessible restore tools.

Support center

Quick answers.

How do I update Masterkit Injector?

Download the latest APK from this official page and install it over your current version. If Android reports a signature conflict, back up anything important, uninstall the old app, then install the new APK.

How do I use Shizuku on Android 11 and above?

Follow the official tutorial for pairing, starting Shizuku, and granting permission. Keep Shizuku active while applying or restoring visual files.

Is the app free to use?

How do I restore the original visuals?

Open the restore tool inside Masterkit Injector, choose Restore All, and wait for the completion message before launching the game.
